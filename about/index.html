HOW IT WORKS

THE ODDS

About 30 minutes before each NFL kickoff, Hindsight saves one book's prices for the game: the winner, the spread, the total, touchdown scorers and player yardage, plus their alternate lines. The prices come from US sportsbooks through the PropLine API, from Hard Rock Bet where it has the game, otherwise DraftKings or Bovada. Each game page says which book and how many minutes before kickoff.

THE MULTIS

After full time, three multis are built only from legs that landed:

  • Sensible: the line, the total, a touchdown scorer and a prop or two, at odds of about 10 to 20.
  • Dream: the market's own lines, with two anytime (or 2+) touchdown scorers and the props that beat their line by the most yards. Odds up to 1,000.
  • Moonshot: the same idea with the first touchdown scorer, taken up to odds of 20,000: the most a bookmaker would actually pay, since $50 at those odds is a $1 million return.
  • Absurd: everything that landed, with no limit. The hardest alternate line and total, every touchdown scorer, and the market's passing, receiving and rushing line for every player who did something worth mentioning. Shown on a $1 bet, because the numbers get silly.

WHY “UP TO”

The odds shown are every leg's price multiplied together. A real same game multi pays less, because a bookmaker prices related legs together (a team covering a big spread and its quarterback throwing for 300 yards tend to happen together). So treat each return as the most that multi could have paid. Returns include the stake.

HOW LEGS ARE GRADED

  • US sportsbook rules: overtime counts for everything.
  • A line hit exactly is a push, and a player who doesn't appear in the box score is void. Both drop out of a multi.
  • Touchdowns are rushing, receiving, return or defensive. A quarterback's passing touchdowns don't count for him.
  • Results come from ESPN's box score and play-by-play, checked again 24 hours later for stat corrections.

NOT BETTING ADVICE

Hindsight is for fun. It takes no bets, has no bookmaker links, no ads and no tracking. Knowing what would have paid says nothing about what will. If gambling stops being fun, call Gambling Help Online on 1800 858 858.
